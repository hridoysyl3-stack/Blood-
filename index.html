    Health & Blood Donation Tips

    Before Donation

    Drink plenty of water. Eat a healthy, iron-rich meal. Ensure you had a good night's sleep.

    After Donation

    Rest for 10-15 mins. Drink extra liquids for the next 24 hours.

    Eligibility

    Must weigh at least 50 kg, be free from fever, wait 120 days since last donation.
