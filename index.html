    Health & Blood Donation Tips

    Before Donation

    Drink plenty of water. Eat a healthy, iron-rich meal (avoid fatty foods) before donating. Ensure you had a good night's sleep.

    After Donation

    Rest for 10-15 mins. Drink extra liquids for the next 24 hours. Avoid heavy physical exercise or lifting heavy weights for the rest of the day.

    Eligibility

    You must weigh at least 50 kg, be free from cold/fever, and it must be at least 4 months (120 days) since your last donation.
