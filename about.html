Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Transparent policies for a safe and secure blood donation ecosystem.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Personal Data: Full Name, Phone (WhatsApp), Address, Guardian details, MISTian Reference. Academic Data: MIST ID, Dept, Batch, ID card photo. Medical Data: Blood Group.

Data Visibility & Security

Your phone number is never publicly displayed. Search results only display your Name, Dept, and Batch. All ID photos are encrypted after verification.

Terms of Service

User Responsibilities

All information provided must be 100% accurate. You will not post fake or non-emergency requests.

Zero-Tolerance

Any user found harassing donors or misbehaving with the patient’s family will face immediate suspension.
