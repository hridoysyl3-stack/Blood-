About Us

The Story Behind MIST Blood Connect

"Our ultimate goal is simple: No MISTian or their family should ever feel helpless due to a lack of blood."

Urgent Need!

The Spark: A Familiar Panic

We have all experienced a common scenario: a sudden, frantic message for emergency blood popping up in our departmental or club group chats. In those critical moments, finding the right person at the right time becomes an overwhelming struggle. Especially in a busy city like Dhaka, for those who don’t have a large circle of relatives nearby, the pressure of managing blood in a life-or-death situation is agonizing.

The Limits of Spreadsheets

Many of our campus clubs and groups maintain donor databases using standard Excel sheets. However, during an absolute emergency, manually filtering through rows of outdated data to find who is actually available and eligible to donate is a stressful and time-consuming nightmare.

The Trust Barrier

Many students genuinely want to donate blood and save lives. But recently, the rise of fake requests, scams, and security issues has made people hesitant. The constant fear of "Is this request real?" holds many willing donors back, leaving real patients in danger.

Our Solution: A Verified Ecosystem

MIST Blood Connect was built as a permanent, secure solution to these exact problems. We are not just another database; we are a verified ecosystem built by MISTians, for MISTians.

Exclusive Access

You cannot simply log in and post a request. Both donors and requesters must be verified MIST students, faculty, or staff.

Strict Verification

From registration to posting a request, our multi-step verification process ensures that only 100% genuine medical emergencies reach our donors.

Zero Spam, Max Security

By eliminating fake requests, we guarantee our donors a safe and transparent environment to do their noble work.
